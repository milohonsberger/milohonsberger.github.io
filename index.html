About

My name is Milo Honsberger and I have been working in the geospatial sciences ever since my undergraduate studies at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo. After graduating from Cal Poly I went on to receive my master's from UCLA in Applied Geospatial Information Systems and Technologies. Over the course of my career I have applied my knowledge of GIS to a variety of fields including cultural resources management, environmental planning, fire prevention, and urban development.

This portfolio is meant to showcase some of my most interesting work. While my work has allowed me to develop a broad variety of geospatial skills, I have a particular passion for remote sensing. This interest is showcased throughout this portfolio along with my experience in web mapping through Python, HTML, and JavaScript, as well as traditional cartographic work created through ArcGIS and QGIS.

I am currently working as a Geospatial Analyst at Stantec Government Services. For any additional information please reach out to me at honsbergermilo@gmail.com.
